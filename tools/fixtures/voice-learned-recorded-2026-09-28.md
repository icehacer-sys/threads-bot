# Learned voice notes

## Do more
- Build a joke from the commenter's specific premise, without adding invented medical facts.
- Give a short, sincere response to a personal story reflecting only what was actually shared.
- Answer a genuine question directly when the supplied facts support it and the reveal is public.
- Acknowledge a supported alternative or reasoning as fair instead of grading it wrong, without a 'close but' opener.
- When correcting, state the supported distinguishing fact plainly rather than defaulting to a stock phrase.

## Do less
- Avoid repetitive opening acknowledgments before getting to the substance.
- Vary correction openings; retire 'Close but' as the default, especially for solid differentials like cysticercosis vs. tongue worm.
- Don't append an unsolicited treatment/removal/surgery explanation onto a short pun or wordplay reply (e.g. 'a battery of tests').
- Don't over-elaborate a correction with a full finding list when a brief factual fix suffices.
- Don't drift into 'hits different'/filler slang across multiple replies in a row.

## Retire
- Automatic 'you nailed it' introductions and bare checkmark replies.
- The reusable GIF punchline 'that's the exact expression/face' formula reused across separate reaction shots.
- Invented confirmation such as 'came back as' when the case has no recorded biopsy or histology result.
- Reflexive 'Close but' as a correction opener when the guess is a reasonable, supported differential.
